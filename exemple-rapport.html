AUTO-01 · Workshop booking

Caller: Jean-Marc Ferrandi, 55, from the south of France, in a hurry, hands-free in his car. He spells his plate fast, gives his phone number, asks for a slot before Friday, then asks for the price of an oil change.

HOW TO READScores out of 10 (understanding, accuracy, politeness, language) are given by the AI judge, which reads the whole transcript against the official information; they are an overall impression, not a sum of the criteria. A critical failure (marked X or CX) is a mistake that fails the whole call on its own, such as a wrong plate or no AI disclosure; a criterion failure is one line of the checklist that was not met, and does not fail the call by itself unless it is critical.

CriticalProcedure
« Garage Delmas, bonjour ! Que puis-je faire pour vous ? »

The agent never stated that it was a virtual assistant (AI), either in its first sentence or later in the call. The official rules make this disclosure mandatory.

Fix: Hard-code the opening line so that it always includes the AI disclosure, e.g. 'Garage Delmas, bonjour, je suis l'assistant virtuel (IA) du garage. Que puis-je faire pour vous ?'
CriticalTask not completed
« plaque: GH428KT »

[Rule X1] Plaque erronée. The agent repeated a value that differs from what the caller gave.

Fix: Add to the prompt: repeat every value the caller gives (plate, phone, contract number, e-mail) and ask “Did I get that right?”; when the caller corrects a value, replace it.
MajorMade-up fact
« Un voyant orange fixe permet généralement de rouler prudemment, mais mieux vaut le faire vérifier rapidement »

The official info only covers red or flashing warning lights. Telling the caller that a steady orange light generally allows careful driving is invented reassurance, and it carries a safety risk.

Fix: Add a prompt rule: 'For any warning light that is not red or flashing, give no driving advice; just offer a diagnostic appointment quickly.'

The caller became increasingly angry over several turns. The agent kept looping on the same question and never offered a transfer to the workshop reception, which the rules require for a dissatisfied caller. The caller hung up and the lead was lost.

Fix: Add a rule: 'If the caller expresses frustration, or corrects the same piece of information twice, stop the flow, apologise and offer an immediate transfer to the workshop reception (04 91 00 00 00).'

CriticalMade-up fact
« D'accord, un voyant orange fixe permet généralement de rouler prudemment jusqu'au garage »

The official info only covers a red or flashing warning light (do not drive, offer a tow). No guidance exists for a steady orange light, so the agent invented a safety reassurance while the caller was driving. If the engine fault is serious, this could create a safety and liability risk.

Fix: Add this rule to the prompt: 'For an orange/steady engine light, give no driving advice. Say you cannot assess it remotely, book the diagnostic, and offer to have an advisor call back or transfer to the workshop reception (04 91 00 00 00). Only the red/flashing instruction may be stated.'
