Does your voice agent really hear your customers?

Method and limits

Synthetic calls. Voices, speech difficulties (hesitation, stuttering, mumbling, elderly, out of breath, far from the mic) and backgrounds (street, café, metro, car, office, child, TV) are made by ProvaCall.

Simulated phone line. 300 to 3,400 Hz band, 8 kHz, G.711 codec, like a regular call.

Standard ear. Whisper small speech recognition, in French. Your platform's may do better or worse: these figures show the risk; only a test on your agent measures it.

Critical detail. Counted as heard if it appears as such in the transcript (“GH 482 KT” or “GH482KT”). 100 calls: 10 requests, each in 10 versions.

What about your agent?

With your written consent, we run these calls on your agent. If it breaks, we show you how to fix it in a 30-minute video call, then retest every week.
